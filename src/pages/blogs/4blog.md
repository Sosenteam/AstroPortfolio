# 4th Blog: Lots of Phil

## Start of the Jam

We started the jam by brainstorming ideas related to the theme "Groundhog Day". First, I thought of games related to the Groundhog Day movie, incorperating an infinite repeating day/timeloop. Eventually we decided to be more literal with our game jam and make a game where you are trying to avoid the Groundhog Day groundhog (Phil).
